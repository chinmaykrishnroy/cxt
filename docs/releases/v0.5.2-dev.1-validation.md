## Retained failures and limitations

During concurrent local validation, a PTY TUI preparation reached the scan time
limit and the optional Gitleaks regression failed after 15.79 seconds with
`gitleaks process failed or timed out; export blocked`. No partial export was
allowed. The same TUI request subsequently reached Ready in isolation; the
scanner's three isolated repeats took 1.13, 1.23 and 0.99 seconds. No deadline or
scanner-policy change was made. Concurrency is a suspected contributor, not a
proven root cause or a fixed issue. TUI review preparation builds the enclosing
Git-root graph even when input paths are narrower.

The earlier foundation report also retains a TUI worker deadline failure.
Passing isolated/full reruns does not erase either observation. Resolver syntax
coverage, MCP budget/omission overhead and Rust impl identity limitations remain
as described in the release notes and TODO. The held-out protocol was excluded
from publication and its task contents were not used in these evaluations.
